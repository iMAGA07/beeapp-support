Privacy Policy — BeeApp

Last updated: 26 August 2026

BeeApp does not collect your personal data. There is no account, no sign-up and no advertising. We do not have a server that stores anything about you.

Your voice stays on your device

The “Learn by heart” feature listens to you reciting so it can follow along. Speech recognition runs entirely on your iPhone using a model bundled with the app. Audio is processed in memory as you speak: it is never recorded to disk, never sent over the network and never seen by us or by anyone else.

The microphone is used only while that screen is open and only after you tap the button to start.

What is stored on your device

  • Your display name, theme, accent colour and feedback settings.
  • Your reading position in the Quran.
  • Khatm reading plans you create, including which juz' you claimed.

All of this stays on the device and is removed when you delete the app. Deleting the app deletes the data.

Network requests

To show Quran text, translations and word-by-word audio, the app requests them from the public quran.com API (api.quran.com, verses.quran.com). Like any web request, those services receive your device's IP address and can keep their own logs. We send them no identifier and nothing about you.

The app makes no other network requests. There is no analytics SDK, no advertising SDK and no third-party tracker of any kind.

Tracking

BeeApp does not track you across apps or websites, and does not use the Advertising Identifier.

Children

BeeApp is suitable for all ages and collects nothing from anyone, children included.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be published on this page with a new date.
