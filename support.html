Frequently asked

Do I need an account?
No. The app opens straight into the Quran. There is no sign-up.
Is my voice recorded or uploaded?
No. Recognition runs on your iPhone during the few seconds a lesson asks you to repeat a letter or a word. Audio never leaves the device.
The lesson did not hear me. What can I do?
Speak once, clearly, while the panel with the letter is on screen. In a noisy room, headphones with a microphone help. If it still does not catch you after three tries, tap “Skip” — the lesson continues.
Which translations are there?
Kazakh (Khalifa Altay), Russian (Elmir Kuliev) and English (Saheeh International) — chosen by the app language in Settings.
Does it work offline?
Quran text, translations, word audio and lesson videos are fetched over the network, so those need a connection. Recognition in lessons works offline.
Can I support the project?
Payments are not connected yet. The Support screen in Settings only records your intention; nothing is charged. We will let you know when it is possible.
How do I delete my data?
Delete the app. Everything is stored locally, so it goes with it. You can also reset progress in Settings.
