Frequently asked

Do I need an account?
No. The app opens straight into the Quran. There is no sign-up.
Is my voice recorded or uploaded?
No. Recognition runs on your iPhone. Audio is never written to disk and never leaves the device.
Why is “Learn by heart” missing on some surahs?
The recognition model hears about twenty seconds at a time, so ayahs longer than that cannot be checked yet. Where that applies, the button is replaced by a note instead of leading nowhere. It currently works on 42 of the 114 surahs, including Al-Fatihah, and on about 60% of all ayahs. Longer ayahs need to be split into segments first — that is coming.
Does it work offline?
Recitation checking works offline. Quran text, translations and word audio are fetched from quran.com, so those need a connection.
Can I do a khatm with friends?
Not yet. Khatm plans currently live on your own device. Shared khatms need a server, which this version does not have.
How do I delete my data?
Delete the app. Everything is stored locally, so it goes with it. You can also reset progress in Settings.
