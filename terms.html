The app displays this content as part of its own reading experience. It does not let you export, redistribute or bulk-download it, and it does not alter the text of the Quran.

Each translation and recitation is credited in the app with the source name returned by the API.

Speech recognition

Video lessons ask you to repeat a letter or a word aloud and check what you said. Recognition runs entirely on your device using a model published by NVIDIA under CC-BY-4.0. Nothing is recorded, uploaded or used to train anything.

It is a study aid, not a religious authority. It can mishear you, and it can accept an imperfect pronunciation. Do not treat its verdict as a ruling on the correctness of your reading — learn with a qualified teacher.
